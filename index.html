❓ FREQUENT QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about joining Intense World, timings, and coaching.

What are the opening hours for both branches? +

Our 1st Branch in Chikkaballapur is open Monday to Saturday from 4:00 AM to 10:00 PM continuously.

Our 2nd Branch in Shidlaghatta operates in two convenient daily shifts: Morning 5:00 AM – 10:00 AM and Evening 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM. Both branches are closed on Sundays.

Can I train at both branches with one membership? +

Yes! Members enrolled in our 12-Month Annual Elite Plan or 12-Month Couple Pass receive dual-branch reciprocal access across both Chikkaballapur and Shidlaghatta facilities.

Are beginners supported by floor trainers? +

Yes! Every member receives floor coach support for form checks, machine orientation, and progressive overload tracking.

What is the one-time admission fee? +

There is a nominal one-time registration fee of ₹500/- only applicable on new memberships.
